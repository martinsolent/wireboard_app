# Wireboard tutorial: narration script

## 01. Get to know the workspace

Wireboard has three areas. On the left are the components you can add. In the middle is the board, which holds your screens. On the right is the properties panel, where you change whatever you have selected. It opens with one blank screen, and your work saves automatically in this browser.

## 02. Drag a component onto the screen

Press and hold on Nav bar in the components list, drag it onto your screen, and let go. If you prefer, you can simply click a component and it will be added to the middle of the selected screen.

## 03. Build up the layout

Keep dragging components in to build a simple page: a heading, a text block, a button and an image. Everything snaps to a small grid so it lines up neatly. You can turn snap off in the toolbar if you want to place things freely.

## 04. Edit the text

Click the button to select it. The properties panel now shows a text box. Replace the wording with Get started, and the button updates as you type. For nav bars, tabs and lists, put each item on its own line.

## 05. Move and resize

Drag a component to move it. To resize it, drag one of the small square handles around its edge, or type exact numbers into the position and size boxes. If you make a mistake, press Undo, or Control Z.

## 06. Add another screen

In the properties panel, under Screens, click Add screen. A new screen appears to the right of the last one and is selected. You can rename it in the Name box, and click any screen in the list to jump to it.

## 07. Add a phone bezel

Select your new, empty screen. Then scroll down the components to Phone bezels and click iPhone. The screen resizes to fit the phone frame. Click Fit in the toolbar to see all your screens at once.

## 08. Lock the bezel

Click the phone frame to select it, then click Lock. Now it cannot be moved, resized or deleted by accident while you build on top of it. Click Unlock whenever you do want to change it.

## 09. Build inside the phone

Now drag a heading, an image and an outline button onto the phone screen, just as before. Because the bezel is locked, dragging on the frame itself moves the board instead of the phone.

## 10. Link a component to another screen

On the first screen, click the Get started button. In the properties panel, open When clicked, go to, and choose Screen 2. A blue arrow appears on the board to show the link between the two screens.

## 11. Choose the start screen

Click an empty part of a screen to select the screen itself. Under Preview, click Start the preview from this screen. A small play symbol marks it as the start. You can change this whenever you like.

## 12. Preview the flow

Click Preview in the toolbar. Your prototype opens on the start screen, without any of the editing tools. Click the Get started button. If you are not sure what can be clicked, click a blank area and the links flash blue.

## 13. Click through and come back

You are now on Screen 2, just as a user would be. Click Back to return to the previous screen, or Exit preview to go back to editing. Pressing Escape also leaves the preview.

## 14. Switch between light and dark mode

Click Dark mode in the toolbar to switch the tool to dark colours. The button then says Light mode, so you can switch back. Only the tool changes. Your wireframes stay white.

## 15. Export screens as images

Click Project in the toolbar. Under Export screens as images, choose Selected screen as PNG for one screen, or All screens for every screen. You can then put them into slides or documents like any other picture.

## 16. Save a copy and share a prototype

In the same dialog, Download project file saves your work so you can keep it or open it on another computer. Download prototype page makes a single web page with just the clickable preview. Remember, your work only saves in this browser, so download a copy regularly.
